Fractional Knapsack

A greedy algorithm where you fill a knapsack of capacity W with items (each having a weight and value), and you're allowed to take fractions of items.

Goal

Maximize total value without exceeding capacity.

Algorithm Flow

1. Compute ratio = value / weight for each item
2. Sort items by ratio in descending order
3. Iterate through sorted items — if the item fits entirely, take it all and reduce remaining capacity; if it doesn't fit, take only the fraction that fills the remaining capacity, then stop
4. Return total value

Example
Capacity W = 50
Item    Weight  Value   Ratio
A       10      60      6.0
B       20      100     5.0
C       30      120     4.0
Sorted: A → B → C. 
Take all of A (remaining: 40, value: $60), take all of B (remaining: 20, value: $160), take 20/30 of C (value: +$80)
Total: $240
