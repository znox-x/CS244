Job Scheduling Problem

A problem where you have a set of jobs, each with a deadline and a profit, and you can only execute one job at a time. Each job takes one unit of time to complete.

Goal

Select and schedule jobs within their deadlines to maximize total profit.

Best Algorithm Design Approach

Greedy — always prioritize the job with the highest profit, and schedule it as late as possible within its deadline to leave earlier slots open for other jobs.

Algorithm Flow

1. Sort jobs in descending order of profit
2. Find the maximum deadline — this determines the number of available time slots
3. Initialize all time slots as empty
4. For each job in sorted order:
    - Starting from its deadline, find the latest available slot
    - If a slot is found, assign the job to that slot
    - If no slot is available before or at its deadline, skip the job
5. Return the scheduled jobs and total profit

Example

4 jobs, each takes 1 unit of time

Job     Deadline    Profit
A       2           100
B       1           50
C       2           10
D       1           20

Sorted by profit: A → B → D → C. Max deadline = 2, slots = [_, _].

Job A (deadline 2): try slot 2 → empty, assign. Slots = [_, A]
Job B (deadline 1): try slot 1 → empty, assign. Slots = [B, A]
Job D (deadline 1): try slot 1 → taken, no earlier slot → skip
Job C (deadline 2): try slot 2 → taken, try slot 1 → taken → skip

Answer: Jobs B and A scheduled, total profit = 150.
